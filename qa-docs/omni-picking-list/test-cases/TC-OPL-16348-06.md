# TC-OPL-16348-06: [Picking List] - Hasil Complete Picking sesuai di Transfer Internal, Stock Deduction, New Picking List, dan status Sales Order

## Summary
Dampak Complete Picking ke menu terkait: barang picked pindah ke outbound rack lewat Transfer Internal, Lost jadi Stock Deduction, sisa jadi New Picking List, dan status Sales Order ikut update.

---

## Environment
* **Menu/Fitur:** Picking List; Transfer Internal; Stock Deduction; Sales Order; Item Stock
* **Environment:** Staging
* **Role:** user dengan akses menu di atas

---

## Precondition Data
* 1 Picking List status Open dari 1 Sales Order, SKU A To Pick 5
* Catat stok SKU A di rack asal dan outbound rack sebelum test

---

## Test Data
* Pick 3, Lost 1, New PL 1

---

## Steps to Reproduce
1. Pick SKU A qty 3, lanjut Review Unpicked, Lost 1, Complete Picking, Confirm & Complete
2. Buka View Completion Summary, catat kode dokumen otomatis
3. Buka Transfer Internal dengan kode tersebut
4. Buka Stock Deduction dengan kode tersebut
5. Buka New Picking List yang terbentuk
6. Buka Sales Order terkait dan cek stok SKU A

---

## Expected Result
* Transfer Internal berisi 3 pcs dari rack asal ke outbound rack
* Stock Deduction berisi 1 pcs, stok rack asal berkurang sesuai
* New Picking List berisi SKU A 1 pcs
* Sales Order berstatus partially picked dan muncul di Incomplete SO Picklist dengan progress 3/5
* Angka di Completion Summary sama dengan isi dokumen-dokumen di atas
