Welcome to **OlshopERP** — a unified, multi-module Enterprise Resource Planning platform engineered to integrate your end-to-end business operations. From the moment a customer order is captured down to the generation of the final general ledger and financial reports, OlshopERP bridges the gap between Accounting, OmniChannel Sales, and Supply Chain management.

This guide is the foundation for onboarding both new internal staff and new clients or tenants, so everyone gets on the same page quickly.

## Welcome to OlshopERP

OlshopERP is an all-in-one ERP system that unifies Accounting, Sales/Omnichannel, and Supply Chain operations into a single platform — seamlessly connecting your incoming orders directly to your financial statements.

## What is OlshopERP?

OlshopERP is an enterprise-grade, multi-module platform designed to be used cross-functionally across your entire organization. Unlike fragmented, single-department tools, OlshopERP serves as a single source of truth that keeps finance, sales, and warehouse operations aligned.

Key characteristics:

- **Multi-module ecosystem** — interconnected core features that reduce operational silos between teams.
- **Tenant / company isolation** — multi-tenant architecture. Each company (for example FAT, Lumielle, and TANRISE) runs in its own environment with isolated data, configurations, parameters, and access permissions.
- **Self-service accessibility** — use the web app, and open the in-app Help Center at `/docs` whenever you need documentation.

## Key concepts & quick glossary

These structural ideas help you navigate OlshopERP smoothly:

- **Company / Tenant** — an isolated corporate entity. Data for one company is walled off from other tenants.
- **Menu** — a single functional screen within a module (for example *Purchase Order* or *Customer Invoice*).
- **Documentation layers** — Knowledge Base, Feature Map, Requirement, Technical, and User Guide. Pick a module → menu → layer when you browse Docs by module.
- **Approval flow** — used on major workflows (such as PO or Stock Opname). Transactions stay editable until they pass Approve/Reject and become final.
- **Contextual help (`?`)** — the help icon on individual menus in the live app. It opens menu-specific guidance without leaving your current screen; the Help Center at `/docs` remains the full library.

## Get started steps

1. **Authenticate** — sign in to the OlshopERP web app with your corporate credentials.
2. **Explore navigation** — learn the sidebar relationship between top-level **Modules** and nested **Menus**.
3. **Use contextual help** — open Help Center at `/docs` for foundational context, or click the `?` icon on your current menu for field-level and menu-specific rules.
4. **[Internal staff]** — go straight into the module that matches your role (see the persona table above).
5. **[New client / tenant owner]** — configure **General Setting** first (company profile, currencies, masters) before recording your first operational or financial transaction.
