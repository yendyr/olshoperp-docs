# Get Eligible Shipping Services

> **Source:** [https://partner.tiktokshop.com/docv2/page/get-eligible-shipping-services-202309](https://partner.tiktokshop.com/docv2/page/get-eligible-shipping-services-202309)
> **Category:** Fulfillment
> **document_path:** `get-eligible-shipping-services-202309`

## Description

Retrieve available shipping services in the US and JP. Package dimensions and weight are optional inputs used to calculate the available options. Shipping fees and delivery times are estimates based on the package attributes used for the query, and the available options may change if those attributes change at the time of shipping.

## Required scope

seller.fulfillment.basic

## Path

`/fulfillment/202309/orders/{order_id}/shipping_services/query`

## Method

`POST`
