# Get Creator Performance for Campaign Product

> **Source:** [https://partner.tiktokshop.com/docv2/page/get-creator-performance-for-campaign-product-202508](https://partner.tiktokshop.com/docv2/page/get-creator-performance-for-campaign-product-202508)
> **Category:** Affiliate partner
> **document_path:** `get-creator-performance-for-campaign-product-202508`

## Description

Returns a paginated list of creators associated with a product in an affiliate partner campaign, together with available collaboration and performance details such as commission, free-sample status, follower count, and video and live broadcast counts.

## Required scope

partner.tap_campaign.read

## Path

`/affiliate_partner/202508/campaigns/{campaign_id}/products/{product_id}/performance`

## Method

`GET`
