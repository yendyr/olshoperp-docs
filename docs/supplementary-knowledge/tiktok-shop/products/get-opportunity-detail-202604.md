# Get Opportunity Detail

> **Source:** [https://partner.tiktokshop.com/docv2/page/get-opportunity-detail-202604](https://partner.tiktokshop.com/docv2/page/get-opportunity-detail-202604)
> **Category:** Products
> **document_path:** `get-opportunity-detail-202604`

## Description

Retrieve the full details of a single opportunity lead by its ID, including market trend data, competition level, and recommended actions.

## Required scope

seller.product.basic

## Path

`/product/202604/opportunities/{opportunity_id}`

## Method

`GET`

## Request Header

Follow this [guide](https://partner.tiktokshop.com/docv2/page/678e3a344ddec3030b238fa0) to get seller access_token. |
