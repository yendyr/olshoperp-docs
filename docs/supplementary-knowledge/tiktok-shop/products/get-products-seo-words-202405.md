# Get Products SEO Words

> **Source:** [https://partner.tiktokshop.com/docv2/page/get-products-seo-words-202405](https://partner.tiktokshop.com/docv2/page/get-products-seo-words-202405)
> **Category:** Products
> **document_path:** `get-products-seo-words-202405`

## Description

Obtain SEO suggestions for product titles of live products (status: `ACTIVATE`) to enhance product visibility.
Applicable only for the US, UK, and SEA markets.

## Required scope

seller.product.basic

## Path

`/product/202405/products/seo_words`

## Method

`GET`
